BEE BLOOM RUSH

Buzz around each flower and leap to the next one before time runs out.

How to play: Tap to launch your bee from the current flower to the next glowing flower.
Objective: Land on as many flowers as possible during the round.
Danger: Spinning thorn stems appear later and get faster as the timer drops.
Bonus: Land near the centre of a flower for a PERFECT bonus.
Scoring: +1 for every successful flower landing. PERFECT landings give +2.
