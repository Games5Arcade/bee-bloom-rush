BEE BLOOM RUSH

Rotate around each flower and tap when the bee lines up with the safe landing zone.

How to play: Wait for the bee to rotate into the highlighted landing zone, then tap to jump.
Perfect: Hit the small gold zone for +2.
Normal: Hit the wider green zone for +1.
Danger: Thorn branches rotate with the flower. Tap into a thorn or outside the zone and the round ends.
Round: You have 30 seconds to score as many flowers as possible.
